1. Getting Started

Ledgify starts with sample data from "Patel Trading Co." so you can explore every feature before entering your own data. When you first open the app, the Onboarding Wizard will walk you through entering your company name, GSTIN, and phone number.

💡 Tip: Click "Settings → Reset to Sample Data" anytime to go back to the demo data for practice.

Navigating the App

  • The sidebar on the left has all modules: Dashboard, Invoices, Khata, Inventory, GST, Bank, Reports, CA Connect, Settings
  • Press Ctrl+K (Windows) or Cmd+K (Mac) to open the Quick Action palette
  • Press Ctrl+N / Cmd+N to create a new invoice instantly

2. Setting Up Your Company

Go to Settings → Company to enter:

  • Company Name — appears on all invoices
  • GSTIN — your 15-digit GST registration number
  • State & State Code — required for CGST/SGST vs IGST calculation
  • Phone & Address — printed on invoices

Go to Settings → Bank to add your bank details (shown at bottom of invoices for customer payments).

⚠️ Set your State Code correctly! If your state code doesn't match your customer's, the system will auto-calculate IGST (interstate). Wrong state code = wrong GST.

3. Creating Invoices

Click New Invoice in the sidebar or press Ctrl+N.

  1. Select Customer — choose from your list or click "+ Add New Customer"
  2. Add Items — type item name (auto-suggests from inventory), enter HSN code, quantity, rate, GST %
  3. GST auto-calculates — CGST+SGST for same state, IGST for different state
  4. Add discount if needed
  5. Click Preview to check before saving
  6. Click Save Invoice
💡 GST Rates for common items: Textiles/Fabric = 5%, Readymade garments ≤ ₹1000 = 5%, Readymade garments > ₹1000 = 12%, Electronics = 18%, Luxury goods = 28%

Recording Payment

Go to All Invoices, find the invoice, click Pay. Enter amount, payment mode (NEFT/UPI/Cash), and reference number. The invoice status will update automatically.

Sending WhatsApp Reminder

In All Invoices, click the 📱 button next to any unpaid invoice. It will open WhatsApp with a pre-written payment reminder message.

4. Managing Inventory

Go to Inventory in the sidebar.

  • Click + Add Item to add a new product
  • Set Reorder Level — you'll get an alert when stock falls to this level
  • Use ±Stock button to adjust stock for purchases, wastage, or corrections
  • Stock automatically reduces when you add items to invoices
  • Stock automatically increases when you record a purchase
💡 Always enter the HSN code for each item. This is required for GSTR-1 filing.

5. Khata / Customer Ledger

Go to Khata / Clients to see all customers with their outstanding balances.

  • Click View on any customer to see their full invoice history and statement
  • The 📱 button sends a WhatsApp payment reminder
  • Mark customers as MSME if they are MSME-registered — Ledgify will alert you if payment crosses 45 days (Section 43B(h) compliance)
⚠️ MSME 43B(h) Alert: If you owe money to MSME suppliers and don't pay within 45 days (or your agreed terms, max 60 days), that expense may not be allowed as a tax deduction. Ledgify tracks this automatically and alerts you.

6. Recording Purchases

Go to Purchase → click + New Purchase.

  1. Select the supplier
  2. Enter supplier's invoice number
  3. Select item and quantity
  4. Click Save — stock will be updated automatically

Purchase records are used for ITC (Input Tax Credit) calculation in GSTR-3B.

7. GST — Filing & Reconciliation

Go to GST in the sidebar.

GSTR-1 (Outward Supplies — due 11th)

All your sales invoices for the month. Click Export JSON to download in GST portal format and upload directly.

GSTR-3B (Net Tax — due 20th)

Shows your output tax, ITC, and net payable. Use this to fill GSTR-3B on the GST portal.

GSTR-2B Reconciliation

Download your 2B from the GST portal, upload here, and Ledgify will show which purchases are matched and which are not. Unmatched ITC may be blocked.

💡 File GSTR-1 by 11th, GSTR-3B by 20th of the following month to avoid late fees (₹50/day, min ₹1,000).

8. Bank Reconciliation

Go to Bank. Import your bank statement CSV (download from your bank's net banking). Ledgify will show each transaction and let you match it to invoices or expenses.

💡 Most banks allow you to download statements as CSV from net banking under "Statements" or "Account Summary".

9. Reports

  • P&L (Profit & Loss) — Revenue, COGS, Expenses, Net Profit. Download PDF or CSV.
  • Balance Sheet — Assets vs Liabilities (indicative)
  • Stock Report — All inventory values, download CSV
  • Customer Statement — Select any customer and see their complete account statement

10. CA Connect

Go to CA Connect → Export CA Package. This creates a single JSON file containing all your invoices, purchases, expenses, GST data, and summaries. Send this to your CA for GST filing, audit, or ITR.

11. Backup & Data Safety

Your data is stored only on your computer. Take regular backups!

  • Go to Settings → Backup & Data → Backup Now
  • Save the backup file to Dropbox, Google Drive, or a USB drive
  • To restore: Settings → Restore from Backup, select your backup file
⚠️ If your computer crashes or is stolen without a backup, your data cannot be recovered. Back up at least once a week.

13. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Ledgify without the internet?

Yes! Ledgify is 100% offline. It never needs internet to work. All data stays on your computer.

How do I move Ledgify to a new computer?

1. Take a backup on the old computer (Settings → Backup). 2. Install Ledgify on the new computer. 3. Restore from the backup file.

Can my CA directly access Ledgify?

Your CA can receive the CA Export file from CA Connect. Direct access portal is coming in Ledgify Pro.

Is Ledgify suitable for manufacturing businesses?

Yes. Use Inventory for raw materials, record purchases from suppliers, create invoices for finished goods. Full BOM (Bill of Materials) for manufacturers is coming in a future update.

How do I delete sample data and start fresh?

Go to Settings → Backup & Data → "Clear All Data". Warning: this permanently deletes all data. Take a backup first if you want to keep any entries.
